Lemon Garlic Pasta

A Quick And Easy Weeknight Favorite! 🍋

Prep Time

10 min

Cook Time

15 min

Servings

4

Calories

520

Ingredients

Spaghetti400 g
Garlic cloves4
Lemon (zest and juice)1
Parmesan, grated60 g
Olive oil4 tbsp

Instructions

1

Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Save a cup of pasta water.

2

Warm the oil and garlic over low heat for 3 minutes, until fragrant but not brown.

3

Toss the pasta with the garlic oil, lemon, Parmesan and a splash of pasta water until glossy.
